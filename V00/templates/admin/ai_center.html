Huong dan vai tro cua AI

AI ho tro gi trong viec van hanh website? Tung vai tro cu the.

🤖 AI lam gi trong he thong?

AI dong 3 vai tro chinh trong viec van hanh website. Moi vai tro giup admin tiet kiem thoi gian va giam sai sot.

🔍
1. Phat hien van de
Quet toan bo san pham va bai viet, tim ra cac van de: trung lap, thieu thong tin, sai phan loai, loi link, hieu suat kem.
⚡
2. Phan loai & De xuat
AI tu dong phan loai san pham vao dung vertical, goi y hanh dong xu ly cho admin, danh gia muc do nghiem trong.
🛠
3. Xu ly hang loat
Admin duyet va thuc thi: xoa SP sai, tat SP kem, cap nhat bai viet. AI ho tro, admin quyet dinh cuoi cung.
💡 Nguyen tac hoat dong
  • AI goi y, admin quyet dinh - AI khong tu dong xoa hay sua gi ca
  • DB check truoc, AI check sau - Van de don gian (trung URL, thieu hinh) kiem tra bang database, nhanh va mien phi. Chi goi AI cho viec phuc tap (phan loai, SEO)
  • Batch processing - AI quet theo batch 10-30 SP/lan de tiet kiem chi phi API
  • Health Score - Diem suc khoe 0-100% tinh tu so van de phat hien, giup admin biet tong quan nhanh

🌐 AI kiem tra Vertical nhu the nao?

Vertical khoe manh la gi?
  • Co du san pham trong moi part
  • Co du bai viet (>= 3 bai/vertical)
  • Khong co parts trong (0 SP)
  • SP duoc phan loai dung
  • Can bang giua SP va noi dung
AI phat hien van de gi?
  • Trong - Vertical 0 SP, 0 bai
  • Thieu SP - >50% parts khong co SP
  • Thieu bai - Duoi 3 bai viet
  • Tot - Du SP va bai viet
🎯 Muc tieu

Moi vertical nen co: moi part it nhat 1 SP, it nhat 3 bai viet phu hop tier (nganh/chung/chi-tiet). Tab Verticals giup admin nhin nhanh vertical nao can bo sung noi dung va san pham.
