Auto Generate Backlink Keywords

Automatically extract keywords from article titles, tags, part names, and zone names to create backlink targets.

Dashboard Keywords Instances Suggestions Auto Generate Scan & Build

What this does:

  • Articles: Creates keywords from article titles (priority 7) and tags (priority 4), linking to the article page
  • Parts: Creates keywords from part name_vi, name_en (priority 6) and tags (priority 3), linking to the part page
  • Zones: Creates keywords from zone names (priority 8), linking to the zone page
  • Skips duplicates (same keyword + target_type + target_slug)
  • Keywords shorter than 3 chars or longer than 100 chars are skipped
