Shop Hub — Trung tam san pham

San pham import se duoc tu dong phan loai vao danh muc (Car, Pet, Beauty, Tech, Sport, Bike, Travel...) dua tren ten + category trong CSV. Moi vertical se tu dong hien thi san pham thuoc danh muc cua minh.

Format CSV

File CSV can co cac cot sau (header row):

sku,name,url,price,discount,image,desc,category
  • sku: Ma san pham (khong bat buoc)
  • name: Ten san pham (bat buoc) — dung de auto-detect danh muc
  • url: Link san pham (bat buoc)
  • price: Gia (so, khong co dau phay)
  • discount: Giam gia % (0-100)
  • image: Link anh san pham
  • desc: Mo ta ngan
  • category: Danh muc — tang do chinh xac auto-detect
