Import san pham tu CSV — tu dong phan loai vao danh muc hoac tao danh muc moi neu chua co

Format CSV

File CSV can co cac cot sau (header row):

sku,name,url,price,discount,image,desc,category
  • name: Ten san pham (bat buoc) — dung de auto-detect danh muc
  • url: Link san pham (bat buoc)
  • price: Gia (so, khong co dau phay)
  • image: Link anh san pham
  • category: Danh muc — dung de tao danh muc tu dong neu chua co
  • sku, discount, desc: Khong bat buoc

Cach import hoat dong

  1. Upload CSV — he thong doc ten san pham + category
  2. Tu dong match — so khop voi danh muc co san trong tat ca verticals (Car, Pet, Beauty...)
  3. Match duoc? → San pham vao dung vertical + danh muc tuong ung
  4. Khong match? → San pham vao Hub, phan loai theo CSV category
  5. Tat ca san pham deu duoc import — khong bo qua san pham nao
San pham trong Hub: Vertical "🛒 Hub" > Segment "San pham" > Zone theo CSV category. Co the chuyen sang vertical khac trong admin sau khi import.
